About Us

ALIANS Neurology

ALIANS has been providing Neurophysiological Intraoperative Monitoring in Southern California for over 20 years. The practice is comprised of doctoral-level neurophysiologists with advanced certification in IONM. Each neurophysiologist within the group takes personal responsibility for their role in helping the surgeon provide optimum patient care.

Our neurophysiologists are licensed allied health providers who are capable of interpreting clinical data for the surgeon in real-time without the need for oversight or interpretation by another healthcare provider. We also have MD/DO oversight neurologists that we work with.

This sets us apart from the standard “remote monitoring” model that many NIOM companies employ and instead offers a direct “provider-to-patient” level of care.
